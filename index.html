About

I’m a designer in Madison, Wisconsin. I taught myself by building websites for friends, then spent five years as the main web designer at Nomad Goods. Most recently I designed emails for Statik and KeySmart.

I like working close to the build, whether that’s in Figma, in Klaviyo or in the browser with the developers who ship it. I also build working software with AI, from internal tools to my own apps. Outside of work it’s cats, video games and anything tech.
